01 / OUR PRACTICE

Every moment has
a soundtrack.

From the first script conversation to the final cue, we shape sound around the performance. The result is a world the audience can feel without seeing the work behind it.

Theatrical sound design

Concept, soundscapes, effects, playback, cue development, and the details that give a production its own sonic identity.

Live production audio

System planning, wireless microphone strategy, vocal clarity, mixing, and show execution for plays, musicals, and live performance. When the room allows, we hide the PA in plain sight so every seat hears the story while the stage stays the focus.

Editing & post-production

Dialogue, music, and audio editing that carry the same care for timing, texture, and storytelling beyond the stage.

SOUND DESIGNER

Gary Ljungquist

Gary is a New York–based sound designer and educator whose teaching spans live production and post-production. His work in theater, recording, and editing includes credits such as Mrs. Stern Wanders the Prussian State Library and The Giggling Granny. He brings a careful ear for dialogue, atmosphere, and the rhythm of a story.

SOUND DESIGNER

Donald Odom III

Donald is a theatrical sound designer and president of Odom Audio Visual. His experience spans more than 30 musicals, including regional productions of Ring of Fire and Shrek the Musical, as well as house-of-worship production. He favors hidden microphones and PA systems, passionate about letting audiences hear every word without revealing how the sound reaches them. His work also extends to corporate audiovisual production.

Different tools. Different jobs.

QLab is a powerful playback and show-control platform. TheatreMix specializes in console automation. Theater Control brings mic and DCA cue planning together with audio playback in one Mac show file.
